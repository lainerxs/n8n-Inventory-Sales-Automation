🏪 General Store

Order online — this is a demo storefront that sends real orders into your n8n workflow.

Webhook settings & how this demo works
  1. In n8n, open the 🛒 New Sale Order (Webhook) node and click Execute workflow (or Test workflow), then copy its Test URL into the field above.
  2. Add products to the cart on the left and click Place Order.
  3. Once your workflow is Active, switch this to the Production URL instead — then you don't need to click Execute workflow first each time.

If Place Order shows a network error: open the Webhook node → Options → add Allowed Origins (CORS) → set it to *.

Prices shown here are placeholders for the demo look — the real price, stock check, and revenue are all calculated by your workflow from the Inventory sheet's Selling Price column. Edit the CATALOG list near the top of the script to change products, demo prices, or the Rice/Cooking Oil packaging options.

Important for Rice and Cooking Oil: since each packaging size (25kg, 50kg for rice; Liter, Gallon for oil) is really a separate stock item, this demo sends the product name as "Perfect Blue Jasmine - 25kg", "Palm Oil - Liter", etc. Your Inventory sheet needs one row per combination, named exactly that way, for stock matching to work.

If this is set, each product card shows an In Stock / Low Stock / Out of Stock badge, read from a separate read-only GET webhook. Healthy stock stays a plain badge; once something is at or under its reorder threshold, the badge also shows exactly how many are left (e.g. "Low Stock · 3 left"), so there's still no public page showing your full inventory counts. Leave the field blank and badges just won't show; nothing else changes. It refreshes when the page loads, right after every order, every 45 seconds, and on Refresh stock now below.

Using a Test URL? Like the order webhook, it only answers once per click of Execute workflow — every fetch after that first one will fail silently until you click it again. For badges that actually keep updating, activate the workflow and use this webhook's Production URL instead.
